01 — About

Curiosity, with a
spreadsheet attached.

The short version: I like questions that end in a chart — and answers that survive a follow-up question.

I'm Ashish — a third-year CSE (AI & ML) student from Keonjhar, Odisha, who got into analytics the honest way: one messy CSV at a time.

It started with a question — "why is the hostel mess bill weird this month?" — and somehow turned into internships, dashboards, hackathons and a folder of certificates I am mildly too proud of. Today I build predictive models, RAG pipelines and BI dashboards that people actually use to make calls.

My rule is simple: if a dashboard can't explain itself in 10 seconds, it's decoration. So I obsess over the last mile — the label, the colour, the sentence under the chart that tells a manager what to do next. I also teach juniors Python and ML, because explaining something is the fastest way to find out if you really know it.

What I'm looking for: a Data Analytics / AI Trainee role where the data is real, the feedback is honest, and the team argues about metrics on purpose.
